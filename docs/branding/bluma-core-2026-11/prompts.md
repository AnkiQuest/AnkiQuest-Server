# Bluma reusable prompts

Use the selected happy master as the identity reference. Aki and Ankilope
are style references when creating a new character. The pose prompt follows
the style prompt. These are the successful prompts from the generated set.

## Style

Use case: stylized-concept. Create one new pose of Bluma, the original AnkiQuest purple cow shown in the reference image. This exact reference defines her identity: rich soft purple coat, lavender swept forelock, two short ivory horns, wide rounded cow ears, cream muzzle and eye patches, dark brown eyes, small cream chest patch, indigo scarf, brown flashcard satchel with a gold buckle, plum cloven hooves and thin purple tail with lavender tuft. Preserve her face, markings, colours, proportions and soft hand-painted digital illustration with warm shading and readable contours. A new pose of the same cow, not a redesign. Match the reference precisely. Exactly four limbs total where the body is visible. Transparent square PNG, 1254 by 1254 pixels. Everything visible must fit within a centered 1050 by 1050 area, leaving at least 100 transparent pixels on every edge. Readable at 64 pixels. No text, letters, watermarks, frame, background, floor or cast shadow. No commercial cow branding. One character only.

## Face

Core face pose: a centered head-and-scarf portrait, looking directly at the viewer with a small kind smile. Show complete ears, horns, forelock and scarf. No body, legs, arms or props. Variation: head level and a relaxed cheerful smile.

## Asleep (sleeping)

Core asleep pose: peacefully sleeping curled up on her side, eyes closed, tail curled alongside her body, satchel with blank flashcards beside her and scarf worn loosely. All four legs folded naturally, no additional hoof. No sleep letters or bubbles. Variation: head rests softly on folded forelegs.

## Happy (celebrate)

Core happy pose: standing upright in a lively welcoming pose, full body, smiling, holding one blank cream flashcard in one forehoof, other forehoof relaxed or waving. Exactly four cow limbs in total. No additional characters. Transparent 1254 by 1254 square PNG. Keep horns, ears, hooves, tail and satchel entirely inside the canvas with an 8 percent clear margin. Clean silhouette readable at 64 pixels. No background, floor, cast shadow, text, letters, watermark, floating decoration or frame. Candidate C: frontal pose with a tiny cheerful bounce, one forehoof holds the card near the chest, the other waves; rich soft purple coat, cream muzzle and small cream chest patch.

## Streak

Core streak pose for a learning streak: energetic and proud, standing upright, smiling confidently, lifting one blank flashcard and making a small celebratory gesture with the other forehoof. Add a few bold stylized golden-orange magical flames curling behind the shoulders and beside the hooves to mean enthusiasm. She is comfortable and unharmed; scarf, fur and card are intact. Keep the flames simple and separate from the face. No smoke, diffuse glow, halo or scene. Variation: card held at chest height, three short flame tongues behind the upper body.

## Sad

Core sad pose: seated quietly, ears drooping, shoulders lowered, big slightly watery eyes and a small downturned mouth, holding a single unopened blank flashcard. Gentle disappointment, still safe and lovable; no injury, punishment or dramatic sobbing. The entire body and tail fit inside the square. Variation: both forehooves hold the blank card low in the lap, head slightly bowed.

## Hopeful

Core hopeful pose: standing with renewed determination, looking upward, a small encouraging smile, raising one blank flashcard with a tiny warm golden spark beside it. Ears perked partway, posture attentive but calm. All four limbs and the full tail are visible. Variation: one forehoof raises the card near the cheek and the other rests beside the satchel.

## Frozen

Core frozen pose: sitting safely wrapped in a pale blue blanket, chilly expression with a small shiver, scarf visible at the neck, cream horns and lavender hair visible, two plum back hooves peeking out under the blanket. Forehooves hold the blanket edges. One small breath puff and at most two bold simple snowflakes. The blanket contains no symbols or text. Variation: blanket draped over shoulders and a small chilly but hopeful smile.

## Neglected

Core neglected pose for a long study break: seated with tired half-open eyes, lowered ears and a slightly mussed lavender forelock. Indigo scarf is rumpled but intact. Satchel sits next to her with one untouched blank flashcard peeking out; she rests both forehooves in her lap. A little dull dust on the satchel suggests time passing. Quietly waiting for company, no injury, distress, damage or guilt. Preserve the warm friendly style. No halo or diffuse background. Variation: one cheek rests on a forehoof, the other forehoof in the lap.
