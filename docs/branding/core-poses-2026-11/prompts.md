# Reusable companion prompts

Use the relevant style prompt, then one pose prompt. Supply the named existing
master as an image reference. Generate several candidates and compare at 32-64 px.
The exact successful combined prompts are in [generation.json](generation.json).

## Aki style

Match the welcome and at-risk masters: orange-and-cream red panda, dark chocolate
paws, warm brown eyes, round triangular ears, indigo scarf and matching satchel
with a gold button, large orange-and-rust ringed tail. Preserve soft hand-painted
shading, thin clean outlines and restrained fur texture. Exactly two forepaws,
two feet, two ears and one tail.

## Anki-tan full style

Create a CC BY-SA 4.0 derivative of Shigeyuki's supplied full illustration. Match
its lightly rough ink outlines and grey/blue pencil shading, blue hair, grey and
black magical-girl dress, blue star brooch, grey gloves and boots. Keep the slim
full-body proportions and hand-drawn finish. Draw three separate blue stars on
the white headband, including the centre star. Credit outside the image:
Anki-tan by Shigeyuki (https://www.patreon.com/Shigeyuki).

## Anki-tan chibi style

Create a CC BY-SA 4.0 derivative of Shigeyuki's supplied emoji faces. Match cyan
bob hair, curled forelock, peach face, stepped black outlines, three blue Anki
stars on a white/grey headband, small dark eyes and flat limited palette. Keep
the head-only pixel-art style. Credit outside the image as above.

## Common output

One character, 1254 x 1254, transparent background, no text or watermark. Keep
all parts in frame with a clear margin. No scenery, floor, cast shadow or glow.
The expression and silhouette must read at small sizes.

## Aki: sad

Stand with a modest slump, lowered chin and ears angled down, holding a blank
cream flashcard low in both paws. Sagging shoulders, slightly bent knees, teary
eyes and one small tear, closed downturned mouth. Discouraged but never shamed.
Keep both feet and the ringed tail visible.

## Anki-tan full: frozen

Stand with knees slightly bent and feet together, wrapped in a pale-blue blanket,
gently shivering while holding one blank flashcard at the chest with grey gloves.
Keep long blue hair, headband and brooch visible. Chilly, patient expression,
small breath puff, at most two floating blank flashcards and two snowflakes.

## Anki-tan chibi: frozen

Worried angled brows, small dark eyes, blue cheek marks, small zigzag mouth and
one white breath puff. A pale-blue blanket edge cups the chin. Two small pixel
snowflakes stay clear of all three headband stars.

## Aki: hopeful

Stand with shoulders straightening, holding a blank cream flashcard in both paws
near the chest. Chin slightly lifted, bright open eyes, small expectant smile.
Keep the full tail and both feet visible. Calm anticipation, without a victory gesture.

## Aki: frozen

Stand in a simple translucent pale-blue ice block reaching the waist, feet faintly
visible through it. Face and forepaws stay out of the ice, shoulders tucked, paws
together at the chest, ears slightly lowered and a patient smile. Tail curves
beside the ice; scarf and satchel remain visible. No shards or danger.

## Aki: neglected

Sit with the ringed tail wrapped around the feet and two paws resting on it.
Ears tip outward and down, eyelids lowered, a quiet tired gaze and small soft
mouth. Slightly rumpled fur and a crooked scarf; satchel at the hip. No tears
or injury. Show low energy while waiting kindly for the player.

## Ankilope style

Match the welcome and celebration masters: golden tan antelope, cream muzzle
and belly, large brown eyes, long expressive ears, two curved ridged brown horns,
dark hooves, indigo scarf and matching flashcard satchel with a gold button.
Keep the soft painted shading, restrained fur texture, thin outlines and warm
palette. Exactly two arms, two legs, two horns, two ears and one short tail.

## Ankilope: sad

Stand with lowered shoulders and drooping ears, holding a blank cream flashcard
low in both hooves. Small closed frown and sad eyes. Keep both feet visible.

## Ankilope: hopeful

Stand upright with ears lifted and bright eyes. Hold a blank cream flashcard
near the chest in one hoof; lift the other slightly in quiet anticipation.
Small expectant smile, without a victory gesture.

## Ankilope: frozen

Sit with arms crossed tightly at the chest, shoulders tucked and a small chilly
frown. Add pale blue frost only along the horn tips and ear edges. Keep the
scarf and satchel visible. No ice debris, glow, text or shivering marks.

## Ankilope: asleep

Curl up on one side with eyes closed and head resting on the forehooves. Fold
the legs under the body, with the short tail curled alongside it. Keep the
scarf and satchel. A peaceful compact silhouette, without sleep letters.

## Ankilope: neglected

Sit with one hoof propping up the cheek, eyelids lowered and ears drooping.
The other hoof rests by the satchel. Slightly rumpled fur and a crooked scarf.
Show quiet low energy while waiting; no tears, injury or blaming gesture.
